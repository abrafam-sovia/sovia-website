Frequently Asked
Questions.

Everything you need to know about transacting securely on Sovia.

Is my money safe in the Sovia wallet? expand_more

Yes, 100%. Your wallet balance is backed by dedicated virtual bank accounts powered by our licensed payment partners (including Monnify). Transactions require your personal 4-digit PIN or biometric face/fingerprint authentication.

What happens if a telco network experiences downtime? expand_more

Sovia is built with automated multi-gateway failover. If an upstream provider (like MTN or Airtel) times out, our system silently reroutes through secondary gateways. If the carrier fails completely, our automated ledger triggers an instant refund to your wallet.

How does the Airtime to Cash feature work? expand_more

Select Airtime to Cash, input the amount and your network, and follow the simple on-screen instructions to transfer the airtime to our merchant line. Once confirmed, your wallet is credited with cash within 60 seconds, ready for instant bank withdrawal.

Do I have to pay fees to become a Reseller / Dealer? expand_more

No! Becoming a Sovia Dealer is 100% free with zero registration or renewal fees. Just download the app, fund your wallet, and unlock Dealer OS wholesale pricing, customer phonebook CRM, and WhatsApp branded receipts immediately.
